ResQView.

A live common operating picture that turns the flood of crisis data — 112 emergency calls, social posts, SMS, sensors — into one ranked, mapped view responders can read in five seconds.

Category: Emergency Intelligence  ·  Disciplines: Data Science · GIS · NLP · Emergency Management  ·  Status: Concept & working prototype plan

The Problem

In a disaster, the bottleneck isn't a lack of information — it's too much of it, scattered across too many channels.

A single monsoon flood generates thousands of 112 calls, tweets, texts, and sensor alerts in the first hour. Control rooms and field commanders must manually read, locate, de-duplicate, and prioritize all of it while lives are on the line. Critical reports get buried; the same incident gets reported fifty times; responders arrive late or to the wrong place. Decisions are made on partial pictures.

How it works

A four-stage pipeline, near real-time

01

Ingest

Pulls 112 emergency calls, social posts, SMS hotlines and IoT sensors into one stream as they arrive.

→
02

Extract

NLP reads each message for location, need (rescue/fire/medical), and urgency — turning "stuck on roof, water rising" into structured, geocoded data.

→
03

Cluster

Reports about the same event (by location + meaning) merge into one verified incident — killing duplicate noise.

→
04

Triage & map

Each incident gets a severity score and lands on the map, ranked worst-first, with sources attached.

Human-in-the-loop by design. ResQView is decision support, never autonomous dispatch. Every flag shows its confidence and links to raw sources, so commanders trust it and stay in control.

Build plan

PHASE 1 · DEMO

Working prototype

Full pipeline running on a simulated flood feed, live map dashboard. Already scoped — buildable now at zero cost.

PHASE 2 · PILOT

Real feeds

Connect a live social stream + a partner agency's report intake. Add LLM extraction and confidence tuning.

PHASE 3 · SCALE

Field deployment

Multi-incident, multi-agency, offline-resilient. Hardened, hosted, role-based access for command centers.

Why it fits the challenge

  • Genuinely cross-disciplinary — data science, GIS, NLP, and emergency management in one tool.
  • Demoable: a judge sees a real, working dashboard, not just slides.
  • Responder-first: built around trust, source traceability, and human control.
  • Credible scaling story for funding without re-architecting.
